# Adjusted effect of a large loan relative to income

Generated by `python -m loan_default.causal`.

Treatment: loan amount at least 25% of annual income (36.9% of 2007-2015 loans). Outcome: default. Differences are in percentage points (pp) of default probability.

These are observational estimates. They equal a causal effect only if the recorded application fields capture everything that drives both the loan size and default (no unrecorded confounding), and only for the binary contrast at 25%. They do not describe the effect of a lending policy such as a cap, which would also change which loans are taken and on what terms. Interest rate, grade and term are not in the data.

## Sensitivity to clipping, trimming and weighting (2007-2015)

## Stability checks (AIPW)

These check that the estimator behaves; they cannot test the no-unrecorded-confounding assumption.
